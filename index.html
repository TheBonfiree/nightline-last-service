Nightline — Last Service

Ten tracks about the last train and the people who are not on it. Recorded between Coldharbour and a stationary carriage outside Banbury.

Tracklist

  1. 1. Marylebone 4:12

    Out now. Opens the record on a platform announcement sampled at 04:40 and never cleaned up. Written by R. Vane. Drums — Ada Foss.

  2. 2. The Long Way Round 3:48

    Out now. Three verses that each take a different route to the same chorus. Written by R. Vane and J. Okonjo. Strings — The Sidings Quartet.

  3. 3. Coach D 5:31

    Current single. Recorded in one take in a stationary carriage outside Banbury. Featuring MERIDIAN. Produced by Halt Sign.

  4. 4. Sodium Light 3:07

    Out now. Named for the orange lamps that were replaced everywhere while the record was being made. Written by A. Foss.

  5. 5. Nobody Meets The 23:50 6:02

    Out now. The longest thing on the record and the only one with no drums. Piano — Elin Marsh. Field recording — Crewe, February.

  6. 6. Replacement Bus Service 2:55

    Not yet released. Finished, held back. Written by J. Okonjo.

  7. 7. All Stations To Yours 4:40

    Not yet released. The slow one. Stops everywhere, which is the point. Featuring Elin Marsh.

  8. 8. Terminus 7:18

    Out now. Ends with four minutes of the room the record was made in. Written by R. Vane and A. Foss.

  9. 9. Ghost Train 3:22

    Cut from the record, listed anyway. Written by R. Vane.

  10. 10. Last Service (Reprise) 1:44

    Out now. Forty seconds of the first track, played back through the station PA. Voice — the 23:50 announcer.

About

NIGHTLINE is R. Vane, Ada Foss and J. Okonjo. Last Service was written on the 23:50 and recorded in eleven days.
